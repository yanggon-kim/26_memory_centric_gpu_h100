# Historical RTX 5080 motivation for the new H100 study

Snapshot prepared 2026-10-16. These are historical RTX 5080 observations and conditional analyses, not H100 data. The RTX study is incomplete; do not infer that every capture, calibration, energy or final-review gate passed. Exact replication requires the original immutable artifacts; this portable narrative is not a raw measurement archive. Original study namespace: shoreline_motivation_study, separate from older resource_trade_study/traffic_reuse_study results. Preserve the older reference as a distinct study.

The accepted four-capture kernel subset was independently reviewed. Subsequent endpoint-qualified nine-capture progress replicates the main trends; its final12-condition gate and correctedB8GEMM captures remain incomplete. Occupancy (~23.81% GEMV,8.33% FA) and warp-scoreboard stalls (~56.7% GEMV) are not decoder-wide compute utilization or wall time waiting for DRAM.

The independently reviewed full-model what-if grid has9eligible case/engine joins,1clock-rejected join and1,152conditional scenarios. Assuming c=.5,r=1.2,f=0 and preserved residual/overlap, Mistral graph speedup lies roughly1.15–1.17x. A losing example c=.5,r=.7,f=.2 remains slower because .8/.7>1. These are assumption envelopes, not measured architecture gains or statistical CIs. Useful-F peak-based services can be optimistic.
