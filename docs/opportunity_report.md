# H100 Phase 0–2 opportunity and stop decision — 2026-10-13

**Decision: stop before physical architecture modification.** The current evidence does not establish a credible, calibrated H100 area/energy/performance benefit from adding SRAM or removing compute. The bottleneck classification, optimized vLLM baseline and whole-model trace calibration remain incomplete. The existing eager archive now has a verified durable transfer. The analytical reuse study gives a conditional research direction, not an architecture result.

## Evidence achieved

- The exclusively allocated H100 80GB HBM3, sm_90, 132 SMs and 50 MiB L2 were queried directly. The CUDA/NVML UUID crosscheck passed for `GPU-18558721-7ba2-20a7-f883-b2c4e2593aff`. Driver 570.195.03 is below the ordinary CUDA 13 R580 requirement of the original pinned route. A separately pinned torch 2.11.0+cu128 / transformers 4.57.6 bridge ran BF16 Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct at immutable revision `989aa7980e4cf806f80c7fef2b1adb7bc71aa306`. It is not the vLLM optimized baseline. Source: `results/environment_cu128.json`, `configs/runtime_cu128.lock.txt`, and each `run.json`.
- Compiled StaticCache B1/C128 smoke and B1/C1024 passed the strict model.generate/eager/compiled token gates with three complete warmups and three measured trials. Median timed decode wall was approximately 164.07 ms for 63 B1/C128 steps and 180.52 ms for 63 B1/C1024 steps. The first generated token and prefill are outside these windows. The B8/C4096 compiled route failed token consistency on its first measured trial in two attempts; the later two trial hashes matched warmups in the preserved retry. It is excluded from performance claims. Eager B8/C4096 did pass and provides a distinct software control. `results/h100_measured_cases.csv` preserves measured rows and NA reasons.
- Eager B1/C1024 board energy used 2047 timed steps per trial, three warmups and trials, approximately 33-second windows. This satisfies the internal cadence/window/agreement qualification only. Absolute sensor accuracy, memory energy and dynamic/static split remain uncalibrated. Raw estimators and windows remain in the source `run.json`.

## Quantified analytical bound and limits

`results/bridge_reuse_64k_selected/traffic_bound_curves.csv` models synthetic full-block fills over the measured 50 MiB L2, explicit policies and B1/C1024 and B8/C4096 shapes. For B1/C1024 at 64 KiB blocks, 128 MiB of extra ordinary LRU cache saves **0%** of modeled read-fill bytes; a selective weight-pinning policy saves **4.30%**. At 512 MiB, LRU remains approximately 0%, while pinning saves **17.22%**. These numbers are sensitive to replacement/residency control and assume an added capacity without SRAM tag, ECC, port, routing, NoC, timing or leakage cost. The 4 KiB and 2 MiB sensitivity files remain analytical. The trace's partial layer coverage does not validate this logical stream. The modeled values are neither GPU traffic counters nor HBM savings.

The B8/C4096 analytical case gives about 3.33% modeled read-fill savings from 128 MiB of selective weight pinning without a shared prefix, versus 0% for ordinary LRU. This remains an analytical negative regime for simple cache enlargement; B8 compute activity itself lacks hardware-counter classification. `remote_energy_break_even.csv` is an **illustrative dimensionless** sensitivity, not an energy model fitted to H100. No positive area or board-energy prediction follows from its break-even fractions.

The positive pinning bound is worth investigating only if future full-model optimized traces and counters establish that the relevant weight rereads reach HBM, and a realistic floorplan/power model shows a better trade than existing Hopper cache or software placement. Ordinary LRU enlargement is weak in the selected logical regime. At larger batch, compute-active counterexamples and existing Hopper features need measurement before considering fewer Tensor Core pipelines.

## Gates that prevent architecture claims

1. **Baseline and correctness:** the original pinned CUDA 13/vLLM route cannot run as specified on the installed driver, and no compatible optimized vLLM configuration was validated. The compiled B8/C4096 mismatch is unresolved; it cannot be silently excluded from a complete baseline.
2. **Bottleneck:** H100 HBM/L2/Tensor Core/issue counters are unavailable with current profiler permissions. Eager timings and analytical traffic cannot identify a saturated HBM bottleneck or prove spare compute capacity.
3. **Trace and calibration:** micro, selected layer and whole-model HF eager capture prove real sm_90 capture/postprocessing/one-kernel replay. They do not prove optimized vLLM kernel behavior, TMA/WGMMA/barrier semantic accuracy, exact-SKU simulator timing, or same-program eager replay calibration. The 1,568 dropped-TRYWAIT warning lines prevent an async correctness claim.
4. **Area and energy:** no realistic freed-compute area, SRAM/NoC PPA or calibrated memory power exists. Qualified whole-board J/token is not an architectural energy estimate.
5. **Optimized trace closeout:** the existing whole-model HF eager archive passed full remote readback after upload to private Hugging Face dataset `yang-gon/h100-memory-centric-accelsim-traces` at revision `55688d40408d0684f1f9d1f458cd070ada4bc588`. See `results/archive_transfer.json`. Any future optimized vLLM trace needs its own nonempty capture, replay and verified transfer.

**Research pivot:** obtain a driver-compatible optimized baseline and profiling permission on an exclusively allocated H100, then measure actual HBM/L2 traffic and representative compute-active regimes. Revisit selective residency/weight pinning or on-chip movement only after full-model native trace semantics and matched eager calibration pass. Preserve all failed/partial runs and do not start physical architecture edits from this data.
