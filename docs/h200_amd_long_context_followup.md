## What changed on AMD

Use these frozen sources for exact generator distributions, scheduler policies, acceptance rules and analysis. Inspect them from either branch with `git show 8c4cc5b9:docs/amd/lc_campaign_design_20261124.md` after fetching the AMD branch. The [design and amendment 7 outcome](https://github.com/yanggon-kim/26_memory_centric_gpu_h100/blob/8c4cc5b9/docs/amd/lc_campaign_design_20261124.md) and [results including the four-worker comparison](https://github.com/yanggon-kim/26_memory_centric_gpu_h100/blob/8c4cc5b9/docs/amd/lc_campaign_results_20261124.md) are the references.

* **Measured on AMD.** At 32K, prefill took about 1.08×, 1.54× and 2.44× whole-prompt time at chunk budgets 4096, 1024 and 256. At decode width eight, attention's measured time share rose from about 7% to 51% between 1K and 32K. Long outputs could leave most timed execution in pure decode. Arrival pattern and the latency objective changed the serving decision.
* **Measured on four AMD GPUs.** Disaggregation protected worst token gaps. Long prompts exposed a producer slowdown and could favor whole-prompt colocation under the primary SLO. A short consumer handoff interval left substantial producer-side time unexplained.
* **Newest completed AMD experiment, ld2 phase A.** The 16-channel asynchronous control took 338 ms per 1K prompt tokens against 195 colocated. Across tested connector settings, p99 handoff stayed at 80–90 ms. Two alternatives finished 30/30 but failed the 10% producer-speed improvement criterion. Several other settings lost a consumer. AMD corrected shutdown false positives. H200 should independently measure producer cost, reliability and handoff.
* **Registered on AMD at this snapshot.** The eight-GPU long-context/long-output worker-ratio sweep (ld2 B), bursty arrivals, restricted consumers and mixed tenants (ld2 C) have hypotheses and protocols. Treat their outcomes as open until a results commit arrives. Record the AMD SHA if using a later result.

## Scoring, failure handling and handback

1. Use the AMD generator, SLO definitions and seed traces at the cited revision as portable inputs. Save trace hashes, model revision, precision, actual SM grants, CUDA/vLLM/connector versions, link topology, cache settings, power/clocks and acquisition IDs. Match scale and model before making a cross-vendor numerical comparison. Record adaptation decisions before judging held-out data.
2. Preserve the primary per-request TTFT and p95 ITL gates. For lclo these are 25 s and 110 ms. Add the maximum-gap gate (1 s, sensitivity 250/500/1000/2000 ms) and tight 50 ms p95 ITL view. For mixdoc use class-specific TTFT (2 s chat, 41 s document). Report completed, failed, compliant and offered counts, compliant requests/s, sustained all-seed rate by the frozen AMD rule and uncertainty. Count timeouts and aborts in the offered denominator. Report both objectives when rankings reverse.
3. Apply the existing H200 replication, placement, clock, isolation and correctness gates. Keep profiled and clean runs separate and join only matching routes and scopes. Check scheduled token counts, no lost requests after preemption, outputs, GPU clocks and time alignment. Label logical KV bytes separately from physical DRAM counters. Record shutdown timestamps, distinguish a live engine abort from normal termination and treat crash-limited capacity as a lower bound.
4. For each contrast explain whether observed time is dominated by delivered memory service, request generation, arithmetic, prefill, transport or queueing, and give the evidence or mark it unresolved. A restricted SM removes several resources together. Label any above-stock bandwidth speedup as a model projection. Report workload/SLO regions where a smaller decode worker helps, is within a declared tolerance, or loses.
5. After every complete contrast push the manifest, versioned launch/analysis commands, raw evidence or verified archive receipt, result table and short interpretation to this branch. Update the limitations register immediately on failure. Leave the next eligible command, prerequisites, estimated runtime and blocked/deferred rows in the manifest before the allocation ends.
